This occurred because Jane was placed in the wrong OU. This led her to have the wrong permissions and

Ticket Number: NMG-0047

Affected User: Jane Cooper(jcooper@NMG.com)

Issue reported: Unable to access HR shared resources. Desktop policies and mapped drives
differ from those of her teammates

Root Cause: Jane was in the Operations OU, which granted her the wrong permissions.
She was also a member of the Operations Group, which was also giving her different access
to things that her teammates do not have. 

Fix applied: Jane was moved from the Operations OU to the HR OU. 
She was also removed from the Operations-Users group and placed into the HR-Users group.

Verification: After checking the HR-Users group, Jane was listed with the correct permissions
alongside her teammates.
